Why College Yard exists

Being a student is already hard enough.

Nigerian students have enough to deal with — lectures, exams, money, accommodation, transportation, social pressure and figuring out life away from home.

  • Finding somewhere to live should not become another nightmare.
  • Finding someone to live with should not feel like a gamble.
  • Getting useful student services should not depend on knowing the right person.

College Yard exists to make those parts of university life easier.

The short answer

So, what is College Yard?

College Yard is a student-first platform designed to make university life easier.

We're starting with practical problems students face every day, and building from there.

The goal is not to build everything at once. The goal is to solve real student problems, one at a time.

What we're building — 01

Find your space.

Campus Cribs helps students discover verified off-campus accommodation.

The goal is to make finding a place to live simpler, clearer and more trustworthy.

Campus Cribs

Accommodation, without the guesswork

  • Verified off-campus listings
  • Clear room types and availability
  • Photos and real hostel video
  • A booking flow students can trust

What we're building — 02

Find someone you can actually live with.

YardMate helps students find potential roommates based on personality and compatibility — rather than simply the first available person.

It asks about personality, preferences and lifestyle to help surface people you're more likely to get along with. Compatibility isn't guaranteed — but it shouldn't be left to luck either.

YardMate

What it looks at

  • Personality
  • Daily routine and lifestyle
  • Preferences and dealbreakers
  • Course, department and level

The bigger vision

This is only the beginning.

College Yard is being built as a broader student ecosystem. The long-term vision is to help students with more parts of university life — but we're intentionally building in stages.

  • Listen to students
  • Understand the problem
  • Build
  • Learn
  • Improve
  • Solve the next problem

Campus Cribs and YardMate are what College Yard is building right now. Anything beyond them is a future direction, not a product you can use today.

Content principles

Six things we care about.

  • 01

    Be real

    Don't manufacture student experiences.

  • 02

    Be useful

    Give students something they can actually take away.

  • 03

    Be relatable

    Talk like a person, not a corporation.

  • 04

    Be creative

    We want original ideas.

  • 05

    Be honest

    Never invent testimonials, numbers, partnerships, users or results.

  • 06

    Put students first

    The content should help, entertain or connect with students.
